Halifax waterfront simulation. Terrain: Service Nova Scotia 2019 elevation/surface products. Buildings: Halifax Regional Municipality open data. Retained licences are in sources/. Shared geographic geometry is used in Gazebo and Blender. Materials are synthetic, buildings are simplified extrusions, and launch pads are authored.

Both drones used onboard vision and obstacle sensing throughout. Only Boreal had simulated GPS input disabled on return. Both reached home and disarmed. Sampled physical path lengths: Atlas 132.88 m, Boreal 118.88 m. Final horizontal distances from their starting positions: Atlas 0.179 m, Boreal 0.101 m. Ground truth is used for evaluation and replay visualization, not navigation. The different corridors and staggered holds mean path differences cannot be attributed solely to GPS.
